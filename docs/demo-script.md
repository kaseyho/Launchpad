# ProofFoundry demo script

Target recording length: 2 minutes 35 seconds or less.

## Human + WebMCP sequence

**0:00–0:15 — The problem**

“Product teams have plenty of signals but very little defensible lineage. ProofFoundry is a research factory where ChatGPT and a human turn fragmented evidence into an idea you can audit.”

Load the demo problem. Ask the agent to inspect state, update the brief, and plan research.

**0:15–0:45 — Evidence enters the factory**

Ask the agent to search the first-party, customer, academic, market, community, and counter-evidence lanes. Extract findings. Open the Evidence Inspector and point out the exact source passage, population, timeframe, caveats, and synthetic-data label. Accept the findings, request evidence gaps, then synthesize insights.

**0:45–1:15 — Ideas are assembled, not improvised**

Generate three candidates and inspect the leader. Point out that Admin Guild scores 98 because every component currently has accepted support.

**1:15–1:45 — Mandatory wow moment**

Say: “Remove community anecdotes and use only first-party analytics, primary research, and recent market evidence.”

The agent calls `review_findings` or `revise_candidate`. Show the community evidence leave the accepted lane, Admin Guild fall to 50% support with two unsupported components, and First-Value Flightpath become the recommendation at 90.

**1:45–2:15 — Attack, then commit**

Stress-test First-Value Flightpath. Point out the counter-evidence and feasibility risks. Finalize the blueprint only after the quality gates pass.

**2:15–2:35 — Trace the proof**

Open “Why Outcome preview exists.” Follow the illuminated path from feature to insight to the worked-examples finding and its DOI source. Export the public-safe blueprint.

Close with: “Raw signals in. Proof-carrying ideas out. Built for humans and agents with WebMCP.”
